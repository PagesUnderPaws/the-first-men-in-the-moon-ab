Two final acts. Read both before you know where they came from.

This edition presents two versions of the final act of The First Men in the Moon. Read them one at a time, respond to each on its own terms, and only then reveal the origin of each version.

How it works

You will answer three short questions about what you already know, then the site will randomly choose which final act you see first. After each ending there is a short response form. The reveal comes only after you have read and rated both.

Please do not search for the ending or skip ahead to the reveal if you want your response to remain blinded.
