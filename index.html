The First Men in the Moon — A/B Edition

Two final acts. Read both before the reveal.

This edition presents two versions of the final act of The First Men in the Moon. Read them one at a time, respond to each on its own terms, and only then find out which version is which.

How it works

Choose whether you are reading here or from a printed copy. You will answer three short questions before beginning. The two final acts are presented in different orders so that the first ending is not always the same one.

Please do not search for the ending or skip ahead to the reveal if you want your response to remain blinded.
